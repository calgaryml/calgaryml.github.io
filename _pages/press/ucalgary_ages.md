_Recognizing outstanding academic achievement in graduate studies_

## At a glance

- **Value:** $11,000 to $15,000 (AGES is normally paid in the fall)
- **Citizenship:** Unrestricted
- **Deadline:** Varies
- **Degree type:** Thesis based students (with the exception of AGES Indigenous)

## Terms of reference

### Eligibility

To be eligible, applicants must be living in Alberta while studying at an Alberta post-secondary institution. In addition, you must meet the eligibility criteria for the specific award:

- Alberta Graduate Excellence Scholarship (AGES)
- Alberta Graduate Excellence Scholarship (AGES) - Indigenous
- Alberta Graduate Excellence Scholarship (AGES) - Indigenous Research
- Alberta Graduate Excellence Scholarship (AGES) – International
- Alberta Graduate Excellence Scholarship (AGES) - Research

### Apply

**Alberta Graduate Excellence Scholarship (AGES):** This award is for Canadian/Permanent Residents. Contact your graduate program early for application information as it is up to programs how they manage their allocations of this award. Programs submit their nominees to FGS no later than June 30th each year for September awards.
