Over the years, the Globalink Graduate Fellowship has supported Globalink Research Internship (GRI) alumni in Canada, aiding numerous scholars in their academic pursuits in Canada. After extensive review, Mitacs has made the decision to permanently discontinue the fellowship after the 2024 application cycle.

This decision aligns with our commitment to adapt our offerings to better serve our participants and stakeholders. The final application opportunity is exclusively available to 2024 GRI alumni post-internship, with limited funding availability. […]
