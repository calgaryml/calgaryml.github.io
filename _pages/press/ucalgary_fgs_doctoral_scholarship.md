Established to attract and retain the best and brightest doctoral students from around the world in support of the University of Calgary’s Eyes High vision of becoming one of the top five research universities in Canada.

|                    |                                                             |
| ------------------ | ----------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Donor**          | Faculty of Graduate Studies                                 |
| **Field of study** | Unrestricted                                                |
| **Value**          | $15,000                                                     |
| **Number**         | Variable                                                    |
| **Duration**       | One year                                                    |
| **Eligibility**    | Open to students registered full-time in a doctoral program |
| **Citizenship**    | Unrestricted                                                |
| **Where tenable**  | University of Calgary, Faculty of Graduate Studies          |
| **Category**       | Open Doctoral                                               |
