### About the RBC Borealis Fellowships

The RBC Borealis Fellowships underscore our mission to strengthen and elevate Canada’s AI research ecosystem. The program provides financial support to exceptional domestic and international PhD students pursuing research across key areas of ML and AI. In addition to funding, Fellows can apply for a co-publishing partnership with RBC Borealis researchers; creating opportunities to collaborate on impactful, real-world research. This initiative deepens the connection between academia and industry and helps advance Canada’s leadership in the global AI space.
