The lab co-organized the [ICML 2026 Workshop on Weight-Space Symmetries: from Foundations to Practical Applications](https://www.weightsymmetry.com) ([archived](/press/weightsymmetry-icml-2026/)) in Seoul, led by [Mohammed Adnan](/labmembers/) with Ekaterina Lobacheva (Mila), and growing out of the lab's research on weight symmetries in sparse training {% cite mohammed2025sparsetraining %}. The full-day workshop featured invited talks from Harvard, the University of St. Gallen, Meta and Google DeepMind, an academia vs. industry panel, and four contributed orals, and drew a packed room for most of the day.
