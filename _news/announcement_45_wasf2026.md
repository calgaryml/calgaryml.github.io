[Yani Ioannou](/labmembers/) participated in the [Wallenberg Advanced Scientific Forum, Autumn 2026: Symmetries in Neural Networks](https://wasp-sweden.org/wallenberg-advanced-scientific-forum-autumn-2026/) ([archived excerpt](/press/wasf-2026/)), held September 29 to October 2, 2026 at Rånäs Castle, Sweden. The invitation-only forum brings together researchers in mathematics, computer science and engineering to work on how symmetries in data, weight space and training dynamics affect training and generalization.
