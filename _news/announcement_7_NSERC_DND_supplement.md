We have been awarded a [2022 National Sciences and Engineering Research Council of Canada (NSERC) and Department’s of National Defence (DND) Discovery Grant Supplement](https://nserc-crsng.canada.ca/en/funding-opportunity/dndnserc-discovery-grant-supplements) ([archived](/press/nserc-dnd-supplement/)) --- one of only 20 awarded nationally!

This supplement will help accelerate our research over the next 3 years.
