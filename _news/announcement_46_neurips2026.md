Our collaborative paper with NYU {% cite lu2026surprising %}, by Jack Lu, Zhenbang Yang, [Mike Lasby](/labmembers/), [Tejas Pote](/labmembers/), [Yani Ioannou](/labmembers/) and [Mengye Ren](https://agenticlearning.ai/people/mengye-ren/), has been accepted at the [Conference on Neural Information Processing Systems (NeurIPS), 2026](https://neurips.cc/Conferences/2026) ([blog post](https://agenticlearning.ai/deleting-weights/), [archived](/press/deleting-weights-2026/)).
The paper shows that deleting fewer than 0.05% of a pretrained LLM's weights matches full fine-tuning across reinforcement learning, supervised fine-tuning and on-policy distillation.
