We have been awarded an [Amazon Research Award](https://amzn.to/ara-fall-winter-2021) ([archived excerpt](/press/amazon-research-awards-2022/)) --- a highly competitive international awarded based on the merit of a research proposal. Our research proposal "Addressing Catastrophic Forgetting with Dynamic Sparse Training" proposes to explore the effect of dynamic sparse training on catastrophic forgetting.
