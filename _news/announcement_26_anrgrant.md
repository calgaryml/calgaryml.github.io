We were awarded one of only ten <a href="https://research.ucalgary.ca/opportunity/2025-canada-france-call-proposals-artificial-intelligence">NSERC/ANR Canada-France AI Research Grants</a> (<a href="/press/canada-france-ai-call-2025/">archived</a>) for our project titled "<a href="https://anr.fr/fileadmin/aap/2025/selection/aapg-2025-selection-prci-crsng.pdf">GHOST: Generative modeling, Heavy tails, Outliers, Sparse Training</a>" (<a href="/assets/pdf/press/anr_2025_selection_prci_crsng.pdf">archived</a>). This project is a collaboration with <a href="https://www.di.ens.fr/umut.simsekli/">Dr. Umut Simsekli</a> at <a href="http://www.inria.fr/">INRIA - SIERRA</a> team and <a href="https://www.di.ens.fr/">École Normale Supérieure, Computer Science Department</a> in Paris, France.
